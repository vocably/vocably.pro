Language: English

Made for anyone learning a language from real content: articles, blogs, news, and videos in the language you're learning. Stop looking up the same word again and again. Every word you save comes back for review right before you would forget it, on your phone with the app for iOS and Android or on the web.

Vocably translates between 109 languages, including 🇬🇧 English, 🇪🇸 Spanish, 🇩🇪 German, 🇫🇷 French, 🇮🇹 Italian, 🇯🇵 Japanese, and 🇧🇷 Portuguese, so you can learn any language from any native language.

＋ Translate Words While Browsing

Translate in context: Select any word or phrase on a website to see its translation, definition, transcription, and example sentences.

YouTube subtitles: Hold Alt (Option ⌥ on Mac) to select and translate words directly in YouTube subtitles while watching videos in your target language.

Several languages at once: Learning more than one language? Keep a separate collection for each language and switch between them in the extension and the app.

＋ Remember Words with Spaced Repetition Flashcards

Automatic flashcards: Every word you save becomes a flashcard with its translation, definition, and examples. No manual card creation.

Spaced repetition (SRS): Vocably schedules your reviews to move new words from short-term to long-term memory.

Mobile app: Your vocabulary syncs to the Vocably app for iOS and Android, so you can review words on the go.

＋ How It Works

1. Open an article, blog post, or YouTube video in the language you're learning.
2. Select a word or phrase you don't know and click the Vocably button to translate it.
3. Save the word to your collection with one click.
4. Review your flashcards in the Vocably app or on the web.

Learn a language from the content you already enjoy reading and watching. Every website and YouTube video becomes language practice.
